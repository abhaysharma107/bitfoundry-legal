Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 July 2026  ·  com.orbitalbytes.bitfoundry

Bit Foundry is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no login. The current version collects no personal data and shows no ads. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

1Information we collect

The current version of Bit Foundry does not collect or transmit any personal information. We do not gather your name, email, contacts, location, precise device identifiers, photos, or files. The game has no user accounts and no user-to-user communication.

Your game progress (money, workers, research, settings) is stored locally on your device only. We cannot see it and it is never uploaded to us.

2Advertising

The current version contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs.

3In-app purchases

The current version does not charge real money and contains no real-money purchases. Any in-game currency is fictional and has no monetary value.

4Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions — no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or access to your files.

5Children

Bit Foundry is family-friendly. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no user-to-user communication, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6Data deletion

Because no personal data is collected or stored on our side, there is no personal record for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes your local save from your device. For any question, contact us at the address below.

7Future versions

We may add optional anonymous usage analytics, advertising, or in-app purchases in a future update, to help improve and support the game. If we do, we will update this policy and the Google Play “Data safety” section before those features ship, and — where analytics or ads are involved — any data handling will be described here and performed under the relevant provider’s privacy policy. In-app purchases, if introduced, would be processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we would never see or store your payment details.

8Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date above.
