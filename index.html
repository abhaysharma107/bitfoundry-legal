Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 com.orbitalbytes.bitfoundry

Bit Foundry is a single-player game. There is no Bit Foundry account and your factory is saved on your own device. The game sends a small amount of anonymous usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics, which you can switch off), registers your phone for game notifications, and offers optional sign-in with Google Play Games. It shows no ads and sells nothing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1Usage statistics (you can switch them off)

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy and where they get stuck, the game sends events to Google Firebase Analytics, handled under Google’s Privacy Policy:

  • App activity: which screens you open, session start, end and length, tutorial steps, and in-game milestones (for example your first sale, a finished lesson, research or achievement). Events never contain your name or your company’s name.
  • App-instance ID: a random ID Firebase creates for this install. It resets when you reinstall. The app does not use the device advertising ID.
  • Device and app information: phone or tablet, app version, and the standard technical fields Firebase adds (Android version, device model, language). Google may derive an approximate country or region from your IP address; the app has no location permission.

Switch it off: Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”. When off, the game sends no events.

2Notifications

The game can show a few friendly reminders, at most two a day and never at night: for example when your trucks have brought in money, a research is done, or the day’s crate is waiting. These are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in Settings (“Come-back reminders”) or in Android’s notification settings, and they only appear if you allow notifications.

To be able to deliver notifications while the game is closed, the app registers with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and sends your phone’s notification token and a random install ID created by the app to our small notification service (run by Orbital Bytes on Cloudflare). It stores nothing else about you. The token is deleted when Google reports it invalid, for example after you uninstall.

3Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides your Play Games display name and player ID to the game so it can show that you are signed in and record achievements. Sign-in is optional; the game is fully playable without it. We do not receive this on any server of ours. A “signed in” analytics event without your name or ID is recorded.

4What stays on your device

Your factory, progress, collections and settings are stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android may copy your saves and settings to your own Google backup so you can restore them on a new phone; we do not receive that backup. Notification identifiers are excluded from it.

5Advertising and purchases

The game contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs, and does not charge real money. Any in-game money is fictional. If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the Google Play “Data safety” section before they ship; purchases would be processed entirely by Google Play Billing and we would never see your payment details.

6Permissions

Internet and network state (usage statistics, notifications, Play Games), notifications (only if you allow them), and vibration (for tap feedback, which you can switch off in Settings). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.

7Children

Bit Foundry is family-friendly: no ads, no real-money purchases and no chat or user-to-user contact. It collects only the anonymous statistics and notification identifiers described above and does not knowingly collect personal information that identifies a child.

8Deleting your data (Bit Foundry, Orbital Bytes)

Bit Foundry has no account, so most of your data never leaves your phone. To delete what exists:

  • Your game save (factory, progress, settings): uninstall the app. If Android backup is on, you can also delete the Bit Foundry backup in your Google account’s backup settings.
  • Usage statistics: switch them off in Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”. Reinstalling the app resets the analytics ID. Past events stay in Google Firebase Analytics only for its data-retention period and are not linked to your name.
  • Notification token and install ID on our notification service: these are deleted automatically when Google reports the token invalid, for example after you uninstall. To have them deleted sooner, email abhay.shar107@gmail.com with the subject “Bit Foundry data deletion” and the approximate date you played. We delete them within 30 days and keep nothing else.
